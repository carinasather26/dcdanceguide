Cover charge

No cover, no dress code Cover prices can vary by night, DJ, and whether you arrive early — it's always worth checking the venue's Instagram for the latest before heading out.

Dress code

No cover, no dress code Dress codes in DC clubs are generally enforced more strictly on Friday and Saturday nights. When in doubt, dress up rather than down — most DC nightclubs turn away anyone in athletic wear, flip-flops, or overly casual clothing on weekends.

What to expect at Dan's Cafe

Dan's Cafe is located in the Adams Morgan area of Washington DC, one of the city's most active nightlife neighborhoods. The venue draws a consistent crowd of DC clubbing regulars and is considered one of the notable Eclectic, whatever's on spots in the DC club scene. Arrive before midnight on weekends to avoid the longest lines — most DC nightclubs hit capacity between midnight and 1am on peak nights.

Good to know

No cover, no dress code; cash only; drinks famously served as a squirt bottle of liquor alongside a mixer can.
