What to expect at Decades

Decades is located in the Dupont Circle area of Washington DC, one of the city's most active nightlife neighborhoods. The venue draws a consistent crowd of DC clubbing regulars and is considered one of the notable Retro pop, Hip-Hop spots in the DC club scene. Arrive before midnight on weekends to avoid the longest lines — most DC nightclubs hit capacity between midnight and 1am on peak nights.

Good to know

Smart casual dress code; cover $10–$20 on weekends; high-energy and notoriously rowdy.
